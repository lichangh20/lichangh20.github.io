I work toward AI agents that learn from experience to solve complex, long-horizon tasks. To advance this goal, I study how to guide larger models with smaller ones (Matryoshka Pilot), train long-horizon agents through on-policy data aggregation (Agent DAgger), and develop the training infrastructure (STACX), data and environments (MLE-Dojo), and evaluation methods (DREAM) needed for sustained agent improvement.

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computational Science and Engineering at Georgia Tech, co-advised by Bo Dai and Chao Zhang.

Currently, I am a Student Researcher at ByteDance Seed, where I help advance the long-horizon agentic capabilities of frontier models.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Tsinghua University, where I was a member of the TSAIL group advised by Jianfei Chen and Jun Zhu.

Always happy to talk about research and new ideas. Feel free to reach me at cli911@gatech.edu.

Software

  • STACX

    A modular system for training and evaluating LLM agents that connects sandboxed task execution, rollout scheduling, and a training engine supporting multiple training algorithms. Verifier-scored trajectories feed directly into model updates, bringing agent interaction, reward computation, and learning into one workflow.

  • MLE-Dojo

    A Gym-style platform for developing LLM agents through iterative machine learning experimentation on over 200 real Kaggle competitions. Executable environments combine code validation, sandboxed execution, and structured feedback, supporting agent evaluation and the collection of trajectories and rewards for training.
